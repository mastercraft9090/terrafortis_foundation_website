About Us

Built on Purpose.
Driven by People.

The Terra Fortis Foundation was created to address one of the most fundamental human needs — safe, stable housing — through community-driven action and compassionate service.

Our Story

The Terra Fortis Foundation was born from a simple but powerful belief: that no family should be without a safe place to call home. We are the philanthropic arm of Terra Fortis Strategic Holdings, established to ensure that our success as an organization translates directly into opportunity for those who need it most.

We focus on building and rehabilitating homes for low-income families across Texas — working directly in communities where the need is greatest and where our work can have the most lasting impact. We are not a distant charity. We are neighbors, builders, and advocates who show up, roll up our sleeves, and do the work.

Our model is simple: identify families in need, mobilize volunteers and partners, and build homes that last. We measure our success not in dollars raised but in families housed and lives changed.

Our Vision

A Texas — and ultimately a nation — where every family has access to safe, stable, dignified housing regardless of their income.

Our Mission

To build and rehabilitate homes for low-income families through community partnership, volunteer mobilization, and compassionate service.

Our Values

Dignity. Community. Accountability. Hope. These four principles guide every decision we make and every home we build.

Our Process

How We Build

01

Community Outreach

We partner with churches, schools, social service agencies, and local governments to identify families with the greatest housing need. We listen first, then act.

02

Family Selection

Families are selected based on need, willingness to partner in the build process, and ability to sustain the home long-term. We believe in earned dignity, not handouts.

03

Volunteer Mobilization

We recruit and train volunteers from all walks of life — corporate teams, church groups, students, and individuals — to serve as the hands that build each home.

04

Build & Dedicate

With licensed contractors overseeing quality and safety, we build homes efficiently and well. Every home dedication is a celebration — for the family, the volunteers, and the community.

05

Ongoing Support

Our relationship with families does not end at dedication. We connect them with financial coaching, homeownership education, and community resources to ensure lasting success.
